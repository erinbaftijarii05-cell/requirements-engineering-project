# 1.Problem and Stakeholders

## 1. Current Situation
* The college has support services like academic support and  careers and learning support
* Students email or phone to get an appointment
* Staff keep track of appointments using calendars and spreadsheets

 ## 2. Facts
* Students may not know when there is a free appointment
* Staff spend a lot of time booking appointments by hand
* Cancelling or changing an appointment is hard
* Staff have different free times and different types of appointments.
* Student information must be kept safee
* The system needs to be easy for everyone to use

 ## 3. Stakeholders
| Stakeholder                | What they need                         | What worries them               | Uses the system? |
|----------------------------|----------------------------------------|---------------------------------|------------------|
|Student                     | Students can  book easily              | Others seeing their appointment | Yes              |
| Support staff              | Set their own free times               | Wasted time, no-shows           | Yes              |
| Admin staff                | Correct booking records                | Records being wrong             | Maybe            |
| College management         | See how busy services are              | Services being too busy         | Maybe            |
| Data protection            | Student infomation  kept safe          | Info being leaked               | No               |
| Services manager           | Booking numbers                        | Not enough staff                | Maybe            |

* Students might want to cancel last minute but staff would not  want that because the slot gets wasted  and makes everyone wait longer

## 4. Goals
The system should help the college to
- show students where they need to go
- show when appointments are free
- save staff time
- keep student info safe
- booking must be easy and straight forward to do

  ## 5. Scope
* In scope:
- seeing the services
- seeing free appointments
- booking, cancelling and changing
- staff setting their times
- users must be satisified
- 
Out of scope:
- Support staff writing notes about the meeting – the system is only for booking
- Services manager getting reports – not needed to book an appointment
- Admin staff booking for students – students book for themselves
