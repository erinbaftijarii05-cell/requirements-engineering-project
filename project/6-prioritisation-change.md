# 6. Prioritisation and Change

## 6.1 Prioritisation

I used MoSCoW: Must have,  Should have , Could have and Womt have.

- Must means the system doesnt work without it
- Should means its important but there's a way around it
- Could means its nice to have if there is  time
- Won't means its left out this time

| Req                              | Priority          | Why                                                                                   |
|----------------------------------|-------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| FR-3                             | Must              | Booking is the main job. Without it there's no system                                 |
| FR-4                             | Must              | Two students in the same slot would be a big problem                                  |
| FR-6                             | Should            | A student can cancel and book again instead of changing                               |
| FR-10                            | Should            | Booking still works without marking no-shows. It helps staff but isn't needed to book |
| Staff notes about meetings       | Wont (this time) | Out of scope. The system is only for booking                                          |
| Reports for the services manager | Wonnt (this time) | Out of scope. Not needed to book an appointment                                       |
