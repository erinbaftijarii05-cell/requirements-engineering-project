# Week 2 — Stakeholders and Elicitation
Scenario: College Equipment Booking System

## 1. Stakeholders
| Stakeholder | Role | Direct user? |
|---|---|---|
| Student | Borrows equipment for coursework | Yes |
| Lecturer | Needs equipment for teaching | Yes |
| Equipment technician | Issues and receives equipment | Yes |
| Equipment manager | Responsible for the equipment stock | Possibly |
| Department administrator | Manages bookings and records | Possibly |

## 2. Stakeholder needs and concerns
| Stakeholder | Need | Concern |
|---|---|---|
| Student | Know whether equipment is available | Not getting equipment when needed |
| Lecturer | Have equipment available for teaching | Equipment not available at class time |
| Technician | Know what equipment is due out and back | Late or missing equipment |
| Equipment manager | Maintain accountability for equipment | Loss or unauthorised use |
| Department administrator | Keep accurate booking records | Records being out of date |

## 3. Unknowns
| Unknown | Who could answer? |
|---|---|
| Who is allowed to book specialist equipment? | Equipment manager |
| What happens when equipment is returned late? | Equipment technician |
| How far in advance can equipment be booked? | Equipment manager |
| Do lecturers get priority over students? | Department administrator |

## 4. Information sources
- The scenario description
- Mini stakeholder interview (role-play in class)
- Existing booking spreadsheets and emails (would ask to see these)

## 5. Elicitation questions
1. How are equipment bookings arranged at the moment? *(current process)*
2. What problems happen most often with bookings? *(problems)*
3. What happens when equipment isn't returned on time? *(exceptions)*
4. What information do you need to record about a booking? *(information)*
5. Who decides who is allowed to borrow specialist equipment? *(unknown)*

## 6. Interview notes
Completed individually – I missed this practical, so no role-play interview was conducted. The points below come from the scenario only.

## 7. What I learned
1. Some stakeholders (equipment manager, administrator) may never use the system directly but still place requirements on it.
2. Each unknown needs a specific person to answer it. It's not enough to just list questions.
3. Asking "how is it done now?" is better than asking "should we build an app?".

**One thing I still don't know:** whether lecturers book equipment the same way as students.

## 8. Candidate requirement
The system shall record the expected return date for each booking.
